LifePointsCounter TCG
A simple and easy-to-use life points tracker for Trading Card Games, built with MAUI .NET. This app helps you keep track of your monsters' life points, status conditions, and effects, making gameplay smoother and more interactive.

Features
Organized Life Tracking: Shows the life points of all your monsters, including the active ones and those on the bench.
Status Management: Track conditions like Asleep, Paralyzed, Confused, and more.
Effect Handling: Keep track of effects like Burned, Poisoned, etc.
Life Adjustments: Easily update life points during Evolutions, Damage, and Healing phases.
Dice & Coin Toss Functionality: Built-in tool for simulating dice rolls and coin flips to help with random game mechanics.

Installation
download the latest release from the Releases page.

Open the application and manage your life points, status conditions, and effects.
Use the dice and coin flip tool for random outcomes during gameplay.
Feel free to adjust the UI elements to match your preferred game rules.
Issues & Troubleshooting
Make sure the UI elements are spaced correctly and visible.
If life bars or labels are overlapping, check and adjust layout properties like Padding, Margin, and StackLayout settings.
